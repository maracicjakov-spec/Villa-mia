01Welcome to Villa Mia

A Place to Slow Down.
A Place to Remember.

Renovated and let only as a whole, Villa Mia sits in a quiet, green corner of Dugopolje, a village at the foot of Mount Mosor, 15–20 minutes by car from the centre of Split.

It has 300 m² of living space on a 2,000 m² property. There is room for twelve guests to be together, and enough space for each of them to find a quiet corner.

02The Villa

Designed for Exceptional Moments

Two floors, five en-suite bedrooms and a living room that opens straight onto the pool. The whole villa is air-conditioned.

Living room

Open to the pool

On the raised ground floor, a large living room leads through glass doors directly onto the pool terrace and the barbecue area. Stone pillars, warm wooden floors and a deep corner sofa make it a room to linger in.

Kitchen & dining

Around the oak table

The fully equipped kitchen has an oven, stove, dishwasher, microwave, coffee maker and a tall fridge. It flows into a dining area built around a big, heavy oak table.

The balcony

A hot tub facing Mosor

One of the upstairs bedrooms opens onto a large balcony, screened from view, with a massage hot tub for three and a wide view of the mountain and the countryside.

Five bedrooms, five bathrooms

Every bedroom has its own bathroom. Three are upstairs under timber beams, looking out over nature and Mount Mosor. Two are on the ground floor, one of them with a door straight onto the pool terrace. A guest WC with laundry completes the ground floor.

03The private pool

Where Every Day Feels Like Summer

28–30°CWater temperature
45m²Heated pool
Apr – OctSwimming season

The pool is heated to 28–30 °C, so swimming starts around Easter and lasts until the end of October.

The deck is spacious, with wooden sun loungers, wooden tables and a covered corner for the hottest hours. In the evening the water glows blue against the warm stone of the house.

    04Outdoor living

    The Luxury of Doing Nothing

    The grounds are large enough for the whole group to gather, and for anyone who wants to slip away for a while.

    • Covered terrace

      A timber pergola with a long table for slow lunches beside the pool.

    • Stone barbecue

      A traditional built-in grill next to the pool, with its own covered pavilion.

    • Garden & playground

      Lawn, fruit trees and roses, with a wooden climbing frame and swings for children.

    • Shade & lounging

      A lounge corner by the barbecue, and wooden loungers all around the deck.

      07Discover Dalmatia

      Explore the Beauty of Dalmatia

      Dugopolje lies just inland from Split, between Mount Mosor and the Dalmatian hinterland. It is quiet countryside, with the coast and the old towns within easy reach.

      1. 01

        Split

        Diocletian's Palace, the old town and the Riva promenade.

        15–20 min by car
      2. 02

        Žnjan beach

        A long pebble beach in Split with all beach facilities.

        15–20 min by car
      3. 03

        Mount Mosor

        Hiking and climbing on the mountain that rises in front of Dugopolje.

        On the doorstep
      4. 04

        Vranjača cave

        A cave full of stalactites and stalagmites.

        8 km
      5. 05

        Klis Fortress

        A medieval fortress above Split with views of the islands.

      6. 06

        Cetina river

        Rafting, canyoning and horseback rides along the river.

      7. 07

        Salona, Trogir & Omiš

        Roman ruins and historic towns along the coast.

      8. 08

        Sinj

        Home of the Sinjska Alka knights' tournament and its museum.
